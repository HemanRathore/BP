Company profile

Six years of getting packaging right

BP Enterprises is a packaging solutions company based in Kosi Kalan, Mathura, with a second base in Gurugram. We began as JMD Enterprises in 2019 and have grown, order by order, into one of the leading packaging suppliers to the industrial belt around Kosi Kalan, Kotwan, Navipur and Barhana.

Who we are

A packaging partner, not just a supplier

BP Enterprises was established in February 2026, formerly known as JMD Enterprises, established 2019, with a clear vision of emerging as a market leader in the field of packaging solutions. The name changed; the people, the plant and the standards did not.

Today we stand among the leading packaging companies serving the Kosi Kalan, Kotwan, Navipur and Barhana industrial region, under the guidance of Mr. Damodar Singh. We serve manufacturers, bakeries, FMCG brands, engineering units and e-commerce businesses — many of whom have been with us since the JMD days.

Our approach is simple: understand what the product needs to survive its journey, then make the most cost-effective packaging that gets it there intact. Sometimes that is a 7-ply export box. More often, it is a 3-ply box with the right board grade and a better internal fitment.

  • Two godowns and two offices across Mathura and Gurugram
  • In-house corrugation, die-cutting and printing
  • Bulk raw material stock held ahead of season
  • A single point of contact for every order
Our journey

From one godown to a regional supplier

Steady growth, built on repeat orders rather than one-off sales. Every expansion on this timeline was funded by customers who came back.

2019
JMD Enterprises begins

Founded as a corrugated box supplier to the Kosi Kalan industrial belt, working out of a single premises with a small team.

2020 – 2022
Range widens beyond boxes

Kraft paper, duplex board, poly bags and protective materials added so customers could consolidate their packaging purchases with one supplier.

2023 – 2025
Second godown, NCR reach

A 650 sq. m. godown at Old Gurugram and an office at Sector 112 opened the Delhi NCR market to same-week deliveries.

February 2026
BP Enterprises

Rebranded as BP Enterprises, carrying forward the same team, plant relationships and standards under a new identity.

Today
3,650 sq. m. of stocked space

Two godowns, two offices, a 14-member warehouse team and a catalogue covering the entire packing table.

Our vision

To be the packaging partner the region does not have to think about.

We want a buyer to be able to send one message and get the right box, at the right rate, on the right day — every time, without follow-up.

What we hold ourselves to
  • Quote honestly. If a cheaper specification will do the job, we will say so.
  • Confirm in writing. Sizes, ply, GSM and quantity — no verbal variations.
  • Dispatch on the date promised, or tell you early if something changes.

What you actually get

Technical guidance

We match ply, GSM and flute to what you are actually shipping — so you are not overpaying for board you do not need, or underpaying for board that will fail.

Stock held for you

Repeat customers can have their specifications held in stock at our godown, so the next order is a dispatch rather than a production run.

Consistent quality

Same board grade, same GSM, same dimensions on order number fifty as on order number one. That is the whole point of a long-term supplier.

Delivery you can plan around

Two godowns means shorter lead times and lower freight — especially for customers inside the NCR and the Mathura industrial belt.

Recyclable by default

Kraft and corrugated material is inherently recyclable. Where it matters, we supply food-grade and virgin-board options.

One number to call

No call centre and no ticket numbers. You speak to the team that packs your order, on the number saved in your phone.
